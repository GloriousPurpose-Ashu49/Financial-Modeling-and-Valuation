**Amber Enterprises India Ltd. – Financial Modelling & Valuation**

Project Overview:
This project focuses on analysing the financial performance of **Amber Enterprises India Ltd.** using historical financial statements (FY2020–FY2025) and forecasting its financial performance for FY2026–FY2030.

Key Areas Covered:
- Historical Financial Statement Analysis
- Financial Ratio Analysis
- DuPont Analysis
- 5-Year Financial Forecasting
- Working Capital and Cash Flow Analysis
- WACC and DCF Valuation (FCFF)
- Relative Valuation (P/E, P/B and EV/EBITDA)

Key Findings:

1. **Revenue Growth:** Revenue increased from ₹3,963 crore in FY20 to ₹9,973 crore in FY25, showing significant business expansion.

2. **Profitability:** Net profit reached ₹251 crore in FY25. However, the net profit margin remained low at around 2.5%.

3. **Working Capital Efficiency:** The cash conversion cycle improved from 44 days to just 8 days, indicating better management of inventory and receivables.

4. **Debt Position:** Debt-to-equity increased to 1.12x, while interest coverage stood at 2.6x, highlighting the need to monitor borrowing and interest costs.

5. **Future Growth:** Based on the model's assumptions, revenue is projected to reach ₹30,509 crore by FY30.

6. **Cash Flow:** High capital expenditure puts pressure on cash generation. Forecast FCFF remains negative throughout FY26–FY30.

Valuation Outcome:

- Peer-based valuation using P/E, P/B and EV/EBITDA indicates an estimated value of approximately ₹2,141–₹2,499 per share (₹2,436 using EV/EBITDA).
- This is considerably below the reference market price of ₹7,770.50 (26 February 2026).
- DCF valuation also highlights negative forecast cash flows, while DDM is not suitable as the company does not pay dividends.
- The valuation model has certain unit-related errors that need correction, so these estimates should be treated as indicative rather than a final investment recommendation.

Tools & Skills Used:
- Microsoft Excel
- Financial Modelling
- Financial Statement Analysis
- Ratio and DuPont Analysis
- Financial Forecasting
- DCF and Relative Valuation
- WACC Calculation

Conclusion: The analysis shows that Amber Enterprises has achieved strong revenue growth and improved its working capital efficiency. However, low profit margins, increasing debt and high capital expenditure remain important concerns. The valuation analysis also suggests that the market price is significantly higher than the peer-based estimates.
